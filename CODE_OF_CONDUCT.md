# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Submarine community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention,
trolling, insulting comments, threats, publishing another person's private
information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Reports should be made privately through the repository's security
reporting channel when confidentiality is needed. Maintainers will respect the
privacy and safety of reporters.

Consequences may range from a private correction to a temporary or permanent
ban, depending on impact, intent and repeated behavior.
